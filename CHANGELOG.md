# What's new

## 11 October 2026

- **Continue** on the Dashboard opens the lesson you had open last.
- A reload keeps the lesson where you were reading, and the terminal as you left it.
- Each topic starts its own lab, even when two topics use the same machines. Starting a lab in another topic asks first, since it ends the lab that was running.
- After **Reset lab**, every window shows a fresh terminal.
- A lab stops by itself after an hour with no terminal open.
- **Open in new window**, the button beside **Reset lab**, puts a topic's terminals in a window of their own. That window now closes when the topic's lab ends, and the button shows only on wide screens.
- A practice exam's clock starts once its lab is ready. A reload, or a look at another lesson, no longer ends the exam.
- Every lesson gets a ✓ in the sidebar once it is done, and a topic is finished when all its lessons are.
- Notes are saved as soon as you click away or leave the page.
- In a quiz, **Show missed only** lists the questions you marked **Missed it**. It now turns itself off once none are left.
- The terminal and diagrams follow a switch between light and dark mode.
- "Docker is not running" clears by itself once Docker starts.
- When Kubernetes fails to start on the lab's machines, the lab now says it didn't start. Before, it opened with Kubernetes broken.
- A checked-off step stays checked off when steps are added to its lesson.
